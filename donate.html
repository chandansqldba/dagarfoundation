Your Contribution Drives Direct Change

When you donate to the Dagar Foundation, your contribution goes directly towards buying seeds for afforestation, supporting educational kits for children, financing skill training camps, and providing counselling and support systems.

How Your Funds Are Utilized:

  • 40% - Environmental Protection & Agriculture Programs
  • 30% - Education & Youth Skill Development
  • 20% - Child Mental Health & Counseling Initiatives
  • 10% - Administrative Support and Local Advocacy
Accountability & Governance

We adhere to strict Indian non-profit governance standards. Donor acknowledgments and periodic utilization reports are provided for all contributions.
