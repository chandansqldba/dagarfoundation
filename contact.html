Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I volunteer for tree plantation?

We run plantation drives every Saturday and Sunday during planting seasons. Simply fill out the form above or email us with "Volunteer: Plantation" in the subject.

Can I teach or tutor children?

Yes! We welcome volunteers to teach weekend remedial classes in English, basic computing, and Mathematics. Both in-person and hybrid mentoring options are available.

How can I verify my donation?

Every donation made via UPI receives an official receipt acknowledgment from Dagar Foundation. Please see our Donate Page for details.
