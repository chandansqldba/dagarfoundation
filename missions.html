Our Core Missions & Focus Areas

Focused on five foundational pillars designed to generate measurable, enduring social and environmental renewal.

Environmental Safety

We believe in preserving our planet for future generations. Dagar Foundation actively works towards environmental safety through community tree plantation drives, groundwater conservation, anti-pollution campaigns, and eco-friendly practices across villages and peri-urban hubs.

#Plantation #EcoSafety #CleanAir #Afforestation
Support Green Drive

Education for All

Education is the ultimate equalizer. We provide foundational literacy, STEM kits, solar study lamps, and digital tablets to underprivileged children in remote pockets, ensuring no child is left behind in the modern knowledge era.

#UnderprivilegedEdu #YouthSkills #Literacy #DigitalLearning
Sponsor a Child's Education

Empowering the Unorganized Sector

Workers in the unorganized sector often face erratic pay and unsafe working conditions. Through vocational certified training, financial literacy, and placement facilitation, we help daily-wage earners secure dignified, sustainable livelihoods.

#Livelihoods #SkillDevelopment #Placement #DignifiedWork
Partner With Our Training Cell

Child Mental Health

Mental health is vital for whole-person development. We organize safe community circles, art and play therapy sessions, and parent orientation workshops guided by licensed child psychologists to build emotional resilience in high-stress neighborhoods.

#MentalWellbeing #YouthSupport #SafeSpaces #ChildCounseling
Volunteer as Counselor

Farmer's Welfare

Agriculture is the heartbeat of India. We equip marginal farmers with gravity drip irrigation, organic composting guidance, and direct farmer-producer collectives to increase harvest productivity and eliminate predatory middlemen.

#Agriculture #FarmerSupport #FoodSecurity #MicroIrrigation
Support Agritech Kits

We collaborate with local panchayats, community leaders, and civic organizations to expand our ground reach.
