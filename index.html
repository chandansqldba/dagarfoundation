Who We Are

About Dagar Foundation

At Dagar Foundation, we are driven by a deep-rooted commitment to creating a brighter, more equitable future for all.

Founded with a vision of positive change, our non-governmental organization (NGO) is dedicated to addressing critical issues that affect our society, environment, and economy. With a focus on environmental safety, education, and overall well-being, we strive to make a lasting impact in the lives of underprivileged communities.

Community Driven

Working hand-in-hand with locals to create tailored solutions.

100% Transparent

Ensuring that every contribution directly supports our active missions.

Making an Enduring Impact

We believe that sustained, well-planned efforts are the key to breaking the cycle of poverty and environmental degradation.

What We Focus On

Our Core Missions

Environmental Safety

We believe in preserving our planet for future generations. Dagar Foundation actively works towards environmental safety through awareness campaigns, tree plantation drives, and initiatives to reduce pollution and promote sustainable practices.

#Plantation #EcoSafety #CleanAir

Education for All

Education is the cornerstone of empowerment. We are committed to providing quality education to underprivileged children and youth, giving them the tools they need to break the cycle of poverty and reach their full potential.

#UnderprivilegedEdu #YouthSkills #Literacy

Empowering the Unorganized Sector

We understand the challenges faced by those working in the unorganized sector. Through skill development programs, job placement assistance, and advocacy, we empower individuals to secure dignified and sustainable livelihoods.

#Livelihoods #SkillDevelopment #Placement

Child Mental Health

The mental well-being of our children is a top priority. Dagar Foundation offers support and resources to address the unique mental health needs of young minds, fostering a nurturing environment where they can thrive.

#MentalWellbeing #YouthSupport #SafeSpaces

Farmer's Welfare

Agriculture is the backbone of our nation. We work closely with farmers, providing them with knowledge and resources to improve agricultural productivity, enhance their income, and ensure food security for all.

#Agriculture #FarmerSupport #FoodSecurity
